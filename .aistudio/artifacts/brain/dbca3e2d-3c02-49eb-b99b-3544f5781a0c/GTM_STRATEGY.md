# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy: Cardios (Egypt)

## 1. Value Proposition (Refined for Egypt)
Cardios empowers Egyptian cardiologists to modernize their practice, reduce administrative workload, and improve patient outcomes through an all-in-one cardiology-focused EHR, seamless lab/radiology integrations, and proactive remote monitoring.

## 2. Target Market Segmentation
- **Tier 1 (Early Adopters)**: Private cardiology clinics in Cairo/Giza/Alexandria, high-volume cardiac centers.
- **Tier 2 (Expansion)**: Mid-size hospitals, private diagnostic centers (as integration partners).

## 3. Go-To-Market Pillars
### A. Physician Adoption & B2B Partnerships
- **Key Partner Network**: Establish pilot integrations with top-tier Egyptian lab/radiology chains to demonstrate "instant result syncing" value immediately.
- **Clinical Validation**: Collaborate with key opinion leaders (KOLs) in the Egyptian Society of Cardiology to build trust and credibility.
- **Direct Sales Model**: Focus on personalized demos and "onboarding kits" to clinics, emphasizing reduced admin time.

### B. Patient Engagement (B2B2C)
- **Clinic-Driven Onboarding**: The cardiologist acts as the "trusted advisor" who introduces Cardios to the patient as a tool to *better manage their specific health condition*.
- **Localized Experience**: Full Arabic support for the patient app and notification system, including local context for medication management and appointment scheduling.

### C. Regulatory & Compliance (Egypt Focus)
- **Data Privacy**: Ensure alignment with the Egyptian Data Protection Law and secure hosting/encryption standards.

## 4. Launch Phases
- **Phase 1 (Pilot)**: 3-5 high-volume clinics + 2 major lab partners in Cairo. Focus on feedback loop and refining EHR workflows.
- **Phase 2 (Growth)**: Scale to 50+ clinics, expand to Alexandria/Mansoura. Introduce RPM module to patient base.
- **Phase 3 (Full Market)**: Nationwide marketing, integration with more diagnostic providers, and feature expansion (Automated Workflow Engine).
